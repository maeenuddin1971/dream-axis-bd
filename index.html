ABOUT DREAM AXIS BD

Helping you move toward your global goals.

Dream Axis BD is a Bangladesh-based manpower, visa processing, travel and tours, and overseas employment service provider, established on 1 October 2025.

We are committed to helping individuals pursue international employment and travel opportunities through professional guidance, transparent communication, and dedicated customer support.

Our services include work visa processing, overseas employment opportunities, manpower recruitment, travel and tour services, and visa assistance for various countries. We currently provide specialized assistance for destinations including Portugal, Serbia, Greece, Malaysia, India, as well as visa processing for other countries based on the applicant's requirements and eligibility.

At Dream Axis BD, we understand that applying for a visa or pursuing employment abroad can be a complex and time-consuming process. Our goal is to make the process easier by providing applicants with clear information, step-by-step guidance, documentation support, and regular communication throughout the process.

OUR MISSION

To become a trusted name in Bangladesh's overseas employment and visa processing industry.

We aim to connect eligible Bangladeshi applicants with genuine international opportunities while maintaining transparency and professionalism in our services.
