About Dream Axis BD

Helping you move toward your global goals.

Dream Axis BD is a Bangladesh-based manpower, visa processing, travel and tours, and overseas employment service provider, established on 1 October 2025.

We are committed to helping individuals pursue international employment and travel opportunities through professional guidance, transparent communication, and dedicated customer support.

Applying for a visa or pursuing employment abroad can be complex and time-consuming. We make it easier with clear information, step-by-step guidance, documentation support, and regular communication throughout the process — for Portugal, Serbia, Greece, Malaysia, India and other countries based on your requirements and eligibility.
